Food Mini Lesson

Creator and classroom workflow designer: Zachary Halaby. Developed and iterated with AI coding assistance.

Vocabulary and a multi-select construction task. The source provides separate Egypt and Spain lesson routes, vocabulary, a passage, checks and a build-a-dish activity. It is an instructional precursor with a different workflow from World Plate and Food Truck.
