Publication boundary

This package contains a newly invented fixture and code that generates and summarizes it. It does not transform or anonymize original classroom records. Do not add real records, school exports, learner comments, IEP material, screenshots or copies of historical tables to this repository, its issues, commits or attachments.

The controls here are deliberately narrow: no real-data import, no individual learner rows, no free-text field, no external service, and an explicit synthetic label in the data and report. Schema tests check consistency, not legal compliance. A synthetic label alone would not make replacement data synthetic.

For any proposed artifact derived from real records, obtain the institution's appropriate privacy/legal review of authority, context, indirect identifiers and all related releases before sharing it. Removing names or choosing a small-cell threshold is not a blanket authorization. Keep historical originals private while that separate question is unresolved.

Official references checked on 2026-11-26:

  • NYCPS Chancellor's Regulation A-820, issued May 28, 2025
  • NYSED student data privacy FAQ
  • U.S. Department of Education FERPA regulations

The accompanying student_data_publication_plan.txt distinguishes legal requirements from recommended portfolio controls. This draft is neither institutional approval nor a legal guarantee. Its generator creates local demonstration files; the reviewed example is published in the portfolio.
