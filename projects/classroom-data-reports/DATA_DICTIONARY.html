Data dictionary and missingness

All values are fictional. The unit is an operational preparation/reporting job, never a student. Periods and workstreams have no correspondence to a real school, class, staff member or calendar.

Field Type and meaning
schema_version Integer 1, fixed demonstration format.
synthetic Boolean true, a provenance declaration, not proof of privacy.
provenance Fixed statement that this fixture was invented without reading real records.
unit Fixed statement identifying fictional operational jobs.
rows Twelve unique period/workstream combinations.
period Artificial label P01–P04; not a date.
workstream device_setup, materials_preparation or report_review.
status recorded, not_recorded or not_applicable, defined below.
planned_jobs Nonnegative integer count of intended fictional jobs.
completed_jobs Nonnegative integer, at most planned jobs.
reviewed_jobs Nonnegative integer, at most completed jobs.
issue_jobs Nonnegative integer, at most planned jobs. May overlap completed jobs; do not add the categories.
minutes_logged Nonnegative integer duration or null when unknown.

For a recorded row, all job counts are known. A count of 0 means known none; it is not missing. Duration may still be unknown.

For a not_recorded row, work was applicable but the report was not supplied; every measure is null. It belongs in the reporting-coverage denominator, but contributes no invented zero to the job totals.

For a not_applicable row, that workflow does not apply to that fictional period; every measure is null. It is excluded from the reporting-coverage denominator and job totals.

The completion rate is total completed divided by total planned across recorded rows. It is not the average of row percentages and does not describe unrecorded work. A zero denominator yields null, rendered as “Not defined (zero denominator).” Reporting coverage is recorded rows divided by applicable rows. Known minutes is the sum of available durations, accompanied by the number of recorded rows with unknown duration.

There are no names, identifiers, school codes, class labels, dates, demographic groups, disability categories, free-text comments or learner outcomes. Extra fields are rejected. This narrow schema cannot establish that arbitrary replacement data are safe: use only the provided generator.
