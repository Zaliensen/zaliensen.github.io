Lunar I Spy: hotspot search and completion feedback

Creator and classroom workflow designer: Zachary Halaby. Developed and iterated with AI coding assistance.

This is the original I Spy application's hotspot, progress and hint engine with its embedded source picture replaced by a new SVG review scene. Ten of its sixteen named functions retain their original text. The review scene is a clearly spaced icon grid, so its visual-search difficulty differs from the original illustration.

Open index.html with scene.svg alongside it. There are no dependencies, accounts, remote resources or stored responses. Sound is optional and off initially. No original image, recording or learner data is included.

Implemented workflow

The source maps pointer coordinates into a fixed SVG coordinate space, tests rectangular or elliptical target regions, and resolves overlapping hits by region area. A found item receives a ring and a mark on its reference card. Progress counts unique items. Hints highlight a remaining target, and reset clears the scene.

The clean edition retains that flow and the original visual structure. The entire embedded illustration was removed; new vector icons, card positions and hotspot coordinates were prepared for review. These generic illustrations and cultural labels are lesson content, not a claim of validated cultural or curricular coverage.

Access and state changes

Partner-assisted buttons let a teacher mark an item after a learner identifies it by speech, gesture or another response method. They expose the labels and are not an equivalent test of independent visual search. Their native buttons support keyboard use.

Repeated activation cannot count an item twice. Reset also clears the partner controls, and a pending completion callback cannot reopen the success dialog after reset. The dialog focuses its action button, supports Escape, and returns focus to reset. Feedback has a status region, sound requires an explicit selection, and decorative motion respects the reduced-motion preference.

Try it

Select a large icon, then select it again; progress stays at one. Ask for a hint, identify another item, and compare the marks on the picture and reference cards. Open Partner-assisted controls and try Tab/Enter. Complete the scene, use Escape, then reset.

Nine browser scenarios verified coordinate mapping, duplicate protection, hints, completion focus, reset, keyboard controls, cancellation of pending completion, narrow layout and absence of external requests or page errors. See VALIDATION.md.
