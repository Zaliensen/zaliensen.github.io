Kinect classroom research proposal

A 2016 proposal explored whether gesture-based Kinect activities could support classroom engagement and learning compared with traditional computer activities. This package presents the research design and its limitations as a portfolio case study.

The recovered evidence consists of seven Word drafts covering an abstract, hypotheses, theory of action and limitations. Zachary Halaby has confirmed this as personal work. The preserved drafts do not establish that the study was completed, and no results dataset has been linked to them.

Read case_study.txt for the problem, design approach, evidence and relevance to work with users. No original classroom documents, participant information, student records or Kinect SDK packages are included.

This is research-design work. It is not a demonstration of a deployed Kinect application or a claim of measured learning improvement.
