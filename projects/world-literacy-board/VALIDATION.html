Bonus/settings regression: November 27, 2026

From this package, run node test_game.cjs (Node built-ins only). The test executes selected public functions with a synthetic DOM and controlled timers; it does not load the private original app. It failed before the change because one bonus advanced from player 1 to player 3, then passed after the fix.

  • One bonus awards two points and advances one player after celebration.
  • Repeated or stale completion callbacks cannot advance again.
  • Dice activation waits while celebration completion is pending.
  • Opening settings settles the awarded turn once; canceling settings leaves that next player selected.
  • Saving settings clears the pending callback and preserves the reset first player, scores and positions.
  • Finished games do not advance; replacing an overlay invalidates its earlier callback.

Six scenarios passed. The source and portfolio runtime are compared by the repository verification command, allowing only the listed documentation-link rewrite and line-ending normalization. Browser observations below are dated separately; no new hardware-accessibility or classroom-outcome claim follows from the timer simulation.
