Return to Animal Habitats

Source and adaptation record

Creator and classroom workflow designer: Zachary Halaby. Developed and iterated with AI coding assistance. Project ownership is confirmed by the creator.

Source family: the recovered Animal_Habitats_Smooth_Reading.zip, inspected as files without running its application. The original archive remains preserved separately. Private recovery records hold its hash and detailed member inventory; this repository does not contain the archive or private source paths.

Source-derived elements in source-core.js:

  • esc, norm, firstLetter and shuffled retain the inspected source implementations. The normalization/first-letter helpers are retained for traceability; the demo uses the shuffle and choice-sizing behavior in its active task flow.
  • habitatChoices adapts the support-dependent answer-count pattern from makeQuestion. Individual-specific exceptions and goal-driven selection are removed. Generic choice sizes are determined only by the visible control.
  • The reading shell follows the source's word-token interaction pattern, implemented with DOM text nodes rather than copying its HTML/media renderer.

New material: the neutral HTML/CSS shell, memory-only response model, three habitat passages, generic question fixtures, support-state labeling, validation and documentation. This is a reduced reconstruction, not a claim that the source's full application was restored or tested.

Removed categories include all roster and individual-goal objects, source-book pages and text, portraits, recorded audio, video, images, media URLs, encoded media, embedded workbook parts, identity-specific name bindings, persistent storage, imports, exports and saved reports. No original base64 payload is included.

The original has additional functionality not present here, including prerecorded narration/phoneme assets, individualized task routing, sentence-completion variants, detailed support codes and spreadsheet reporting. Those features would need separate data-free reconstruction before adding them to this neutral demo. Third-party media and book rights are separate from ownership of the classroom workflow and remain outside this package.
