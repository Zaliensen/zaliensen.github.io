Public synthetic workbook

The downloadable workbook contains invented values. Historical gradebooks and student records are not distributed; the case study describes the recovered averaging workflow.

This case study concerns a practical Excel grade-recording workflow preserved from 2015–2016. The original workbook uses row and column averages to summarize classroom activity records. Zachary Halaby has confirmed it as personal work.

Open synthetic_gradebook.xlsx to try a new, compact demonstration of the averaging pattern. Every learner label and score is invented. The demonstration contains six fictional learners, nineteen activities, editable score cells, per-learner averages and per-activity averages. It is not a copy of the original workbook, its records, its layout or its grading scale.

Read case_study.txt for the historical evidence, the reconstruction choices and validation limits. The original workbook and all identifiable classroom data remain private.

Quick demonstration

  1. Open the Synthetic gradebook worksheet.
  2. Change a yellow input cell in B7:T12.
  3. Observe the learner average, activity average and overall average update.
  4. Clear a score to represent an unrecorded observation. Enter 0 to represent a recorded zero. These have different meanings.

The invented scale is 0 to 4. It is only a demonstration and does not describe the original school's grading policy.
