    Teacher plan • 40 minutes • setup and access options

    Before class: Open this HTML in a modern browser on the board computer. Choose your recording above if available, check volume, and prepare bells or shakers. No internet connection is needed. Test the board’s touch input. This lesson uses an approximate grade 1–3 level; model every task before inviting individual turns.

    Turn routine: Invite one student, have the class think or echo, let the student tap a choice, then discuss the feedback. Use “Next student” to reset or change the question. No names or scores are stored. Choose students equitably; one student need not complete every task. Suggested minutes total exactly 40; timers are reminders, not a guarantee of participation time.

    Phase / timeBoard taskTeaching and class response

    Access: Students may point, use a partner, use keyboard Tab/Enter, or direct an adult to tap. For non-speaking students, accept gestures and picture choices. Replace standing movement with seated tapping. “Less motion” stops continuous decorative animation. The teaching point stays visible as you scroll. Timers can pause while a student responds.

    Song-learning focus: Listen to a short section at the opening; practice a short phrase in the sequence activity; build two phrases in rehearsal; sing or participate in the final performance. Use your own recording or live voice. The rhyme and math examples are classroom extensions connected to the title and bell theme, not quotations from the lyrics.
