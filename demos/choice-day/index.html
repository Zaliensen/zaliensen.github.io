Classroom activity / flexible response

Choice Day

An adult-supported activity that separates modelling, a learner’s response and the decision to move on.

Portfolio reconstruction · Synthetic content · Nothing is recorded or sent

Teacher controls

What each adaptation does

More support offers two response choices and a counting cue. Standard adds a third choice. Challenge asks for a typed count.

Modelling never records a learner response. The teacher confirms a response and advances separately. There is no countdown or automatic transition.

AAC, pointing, partner-assisted selection, help and passing are valid ways to participate.
