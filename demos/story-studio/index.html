Choose with a click, Tab and Enter, or a partner's help.

Adapt the activity

When enabled, focus the choice area and use Space to move the highlight; Enter selects it. This is manual scanning, not automatic timed scanning.

Teaching notes

A partner may click for a learner using pointing, eye direction or AAC. Confirm the choice before moving on. Passing is a valid response.

The full recovered app contains gaze support; this demo does not use a camera or implement gaze tracking.
