Definitions and limits

A job is an invented unit of preparation/reporting work. Reviewed jobs are a subset of completed jobs. Issue jobs may overlap completed jobs and must not be added to completion counts. This sample has no demographic or disability groups, small-cell student summaries, free-text comments, IEP fields or learner IDs.

The schema rejects extra fields but cannot certify that any future input is safe or synthetic. Do not substitute real records. This is a synthetic portfolio demonstration, not a student decision tool.
